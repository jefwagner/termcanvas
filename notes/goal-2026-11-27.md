`~/.pi/agent/skills/project-summary/SKILL.md` (read it). Roughly:

- *What it is*: `termcanvas` is a family of terminal apps on shared library
  crates — a half-block pixel canvas, a terminal lifecycle/render loop, and the
  apps built on them.
- *The question*: whether a small set of library crates can make terminal apps
  that look graphical (real pixels, real colors) while keeping terminal
